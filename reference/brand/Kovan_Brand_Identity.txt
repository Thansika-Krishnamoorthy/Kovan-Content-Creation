Kovan Labs Brand Identity Kit

1. Brand Core

Personality

Innovative: Forward-thinking and cutting-edge.

Trustworthy: Reliable and professional.

Collaborative: Engaging and interactive.

Voice & Tone

Style: Professional yet approachable; clear, direct, and results-oriented.  

Design Philosophy: Minimalist, modern, user-centric, and intuitive. Balances creativity with clarity.

2. Visual Identity

Logo Usage

Placement: Preferred positions are top left, bottom left, or bottom right.

Horizontal Logo: Best used at the top.

Vertical Logo: Best used at the bottom.

Clear Space: Maintain a minimum of 48px clear space around the logo.

Minimum Size: Minimum width 250px – 300px (height proportionate).

Restrictions:

Do not alter colors, stretch, apply effects, or add elements.

Do not place over complex or distracting backgrounds.

Use monochrome variants only when necessary for contrast.

Color Palette

Primary Brand Colors

Red: #EF3829

Green: #00A95C

Yellow: #FDB917

Blue: #0076BE

Note: Gradients must align with these brand colors and maintain visual clarity.

Typography Colors

Light Backgrounds:

Title: #000000 (Black)

Subtitles & Body: #808080 (Gray)

Dark Backgrounds:

Title & Body: #FFFFFF (White)

Contrast Standards (WCAG Compliance)

Standard Text: Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio.

Large/Bold Text (18pt+): Minimum 3:1 contrast ratio.

Typography

Heading Font: Poppins

Body Font: Inter

Styling:

Casing: Sentence case is preferred for consistency.

Avoid: Decorative or overly stylized fonts.

Spacing:

Headlines & Subtitles: Minimum 8px spacing.

Body Text & Elements: Minimum 16px spacing.

3. Design System & Layout

Grid & Spacing

A structured grid system is required to enhance visual alignment.

Margins: Minimum 52px on all sides.

Gutter Spacing: 20px.

Alignment

Titles/Headings: Left-aligned or center-aligned.

Subtitles: Must follow the same alignment as the title.

Body Text: Generally left-aligned for readability; justified for formal documents or text blocks.

Shapes & Elements

Style: All shapes and graphic elements must align with the brand’s design system.

Color Usage: Use only brand colors or brand-aligned gradients.

Consistency: Graphics, illustrations, and patterns should maintain uniformity across all media.

4. Imagery Guidelines

Visual Style

Atmosphere: Clean, professional, and authentic.

Quality: High-resolution images are mandatory to ensure clarity.

Grading: Maintain a consistent color grading style across all visuals.

Dos

Layouts: Minimalist and structured; prioritize clarity.

Backgrounds: Solid colors or subtle gradients.

Subjects: Professional headshots and real office imagery to show credibility.

Data: Simple infographics and data visuals for explaining services or results.

Don'ts

Avoid: Overly decorative backgrounds or clutter (Professional > Flashy).

Avoid: Low-contrast text that blends into images.

Avoid: Casual, over-stylized, or unrelated stock photography.
